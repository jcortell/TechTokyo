WHAT

The Technology Exchange and Collaboration Hub in Tokyo (TECH-Tokyo G.K.) is the Japanese subsidiary of the Public Benefit Corporation International Innovation Programs Co, dedicated to connecting the world’s top universities with leading corporations. Our aim is to foster robust collaborations through licensing, partnerships, investments into university spinoffs, and sponsored research, as well as to facilitate academic exchange and executive education.

HOW

Bringing faculty, researchers, and entrepreneurs—from some of the top universities in the world—and Japanese corporations, entrepreneurs, investors, and universities, closer together. Exploring academic, partnering, and commercial opportunities through a multi-year program of presentations, lectures, workshops, networking, curated technology selection, competitions and more.

WHERE

Attend lectures and presentations with simultaneous Japanese-English translation in Shibuya-Tokyo, and collaborate in real time with faculty, entrepreneurs, and researchers either in person or using a full-size real-time video portal.
